## TECHNICAL SUMMARY

The paper proposes **Curator (arxiv:2401.07119)**, an in-memory ANN index for **multi-tenant vector databases**. The core structure is a shared **Global Clustering Tree (GCT)** plus tenant-specific **Tenant Clustering Trees (TCTs)** encoded as subtrees of the GCT (§3, Fig. 4). The GCT is a hierarchical k-means tree trained once on a representative training set; “the structure of GCT remains fixed as vectors are inserted to or deleted from the index” (§4.2).
